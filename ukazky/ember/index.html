(03) How we roast

Temperature24 °C
Roast time00:00
24°
01 / 04

Green beans

Our beans arrive in jute sacks straight from the farms. They're hard, green and smell more of fresh hay than of coffee.

02 / 04

Drying

For the first five minutes the beans lose their moisture and turn yellow. The whole roastery smells of freshly baked bread.

03 / 04

First crack

At around 196 °C the beans audibly crack and swell by almost half. From here on, every second counts.

04 / 04

Develop & rest

We let the sugars caramelise a little longer, then cool the beans fast. They rest for two days before they ship.
